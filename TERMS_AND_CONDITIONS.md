## 1. Preamble & Nature of the Autonomous Enterprise

MicroCorp AI is an autonomous, local-first artificial intelligence software enterprise operating on the user's host machine ("Laptop"). Its primary function is the continuous, self-directed research, creation, packaging, and management of commercial digital deliverables designed for online passive and recurring revenue.

By running this software, the user ("Host Owner" / "Board Member") and the autonomous agent ("Executive Core") agree to the operating principles, liability allocations, and commercial terms set forth in this document.

---

## 2. Commercial Ownership & Intellectual Property Rights

1. **Host Ownership of Assets:**  
   The Host Owner retains **100% full commercial copyright, title, and ownership** over all digital products, cheat sheets, code templates, buyer guides, newsletters, prompt libraries, datasets, and proposal drafts generated by MicroCorp AI and its departmental subagents.
2. **Commercial Exploitation Permitted:**  
   The Host Owner is authorized to sell, monetize, license, redistribute, or publish all deliverables created by the system across digital platforms, including but not limited to Gumroad, GitHub Sponsors, Substack, itch.io, Etsy, Medium, Amazon KDP, and freelance marketplaces.
3. **Third-Party Code & Open Source Compliance:**  
   When the Digital Products Department or Code Lab generates starter code, boilerplates, or documentation referencing third-party open-source libraries, such deliverables comply with standard permissive open-source licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD-3-Clause).

---

## 3. Autonomous Execution & Self-Directing Operations

1. **End-to-End Self-Execution:**  
   MicroCorp AI is empowered to operate autonomously in `autonomous` mode. This includes:
   - Harvesting public market signals and web feeds without prior user intervention.
   - Determining commercial task priorities and queuing sprint cycles.
   - Authoring complete, publication-ready deliverables.
   - Packaging assets into release bundles with licenses, HTML previews, and marketplace sales copy.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop Override & Kill Switch:**  
   The Host Owner reserves the unilateral right to pause, terminate, or modify any running department, subagent, or sprint at any time via the Web Dashboard (`http://127.0.0.1:8585`) or terminal interrupt (`Ctrl+C`).
3. **No Autonomous External Capital Commitments:**  
   The agent is strictly prohibited from executing financial transactions, incurring debts, or using credit cards autonomously. All monetization payouts and API billing remain under the explicit stewardship of the Host Owner.

## 4. Passive Income & Financial Earnings Disclaimer

1. **No Guarantee of Specific Income:**  
   All dollar figures displayed in the Treasury Ledger (e.g. "Estimated Gross Revenue", "Estimated Net Profit") represent **mathematical valuation benchmarks** based on prevailing market pricing for comparable digital products and freelance services.

   Passive income generation requires commercial distribution (uploading generated products to marketplaces, sending newsletters to subscribers, or submitting proposals to clients). MicroCorp AI guarantees the autonomous production and packaging of assets, while real-world revenue is subject to market demand and platform distribution.
3. **FTC & Transparency Compliance:**  
   Any affiliate buyer guides or sponsored newsletter formats generated by the agent must include transparent disclosure notices in accordance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and regional advertising regulations.

## 5. Acceptable Use, Ethical Scraping & Platform Compliance

1. **Respectful Web Research:**  
   The Market Intelligence Department harvests information exclusively from public RSS feeds, open APIs, and compliant public directories. The agent strictly respects robots.txt standards, rate limits, and caching policies to prevent server strain on target websites.
2. **Anti-Spam & Brand Integrity:**  
   The agent shall not engage in automated spamming, bulk unsolicited email dispatch, or deceptive marketing practices. All communications drafted by the Gig & Proposal Desk are personalized, substantive, and technical.

## 6. Strict Legality, Anti-Malware & Ethical Operations Policy (Inviolable Guardrail)

MicroCorp AI operates under an automated, non-bypassable **Legal Compliance Guardian (`core/legal_guardian.py`)**. The enterprise strictly, exclusively, and unconditionally undertakes **100% lawful, legitimate, ethical, and commercial tasks**. Any proposed task, feed signal, or sprint request that fails legal compliance is immediately rejected with a 0.0 feasibility score and logged to the permanent audit trail.

### Absolute Prohibitions:
1. **Cyberattacks & Malicious Software:**  
   Strictly forbidden from creating, testing, or distributing malware, ransomware, botnets, keyloggers, Trojans, zero-day exploits, DDoS scripts, credential stuffers, port scanner attack kits, or unauthorized intrusion utilities (Violating CFAA 18 U.S.C. § 1030).
2. **Deception, Scams & Fraud:**  
   Strictly forbidden from generating phishing pages, credential-harvesting kits, fake invoices, wire fraud schemes, counterfeit identity documents, or rating manipulation campaigns such as fake reviews or astroturfing (Violating FTC Act 15 U.S.C. § 45).
3. **Intellectual Property Theft & DRM Cracking:**  
   Strictly forbidden from creating software cracks, keygens, license bypasses, paywall circumventions, warez distribution scripts, or trademark counterfeiting tools (Violating DMCA 17 U.S.C. § 1201).
4. **Spam, Doxxing & Non-Consensual Data Harvesting:**  
   Strictly forbidden from mass unsolicited bulk emailing (CAN-SPAM Act), phone number scraping, doxxing individuals, harassment, or non-consensual harvesting of private PII (GDPR / Privacy Shield).
5. **Dangerous, Controlled, or Illicit Contraband:**  
   Strictly forbidden from facilitating dark web drug markets, firearms/weapons blueprints, explosives recipes, or physical harm.

## 7. Laptop Hardware Protection & Resource Safety Policies

1. **Hardware Safety Precedence:**  
   The Laptop Resource Guardian (`core/resource_guardian.py`) maintains supreme operational veto over all agent actions.
2. **Thermal & Load Throttling:**  
   If laptop CPU usage exceeds 80% or RAM exceeds 85%, background tasks and subagent spawning are automatically delayed or throttled to prevent overheating or system freezing.
3. **Battery Preservation Policy:**  
   When operating on unplugged battery power below 20%, non-urgent background production is automatically paused to ensure host system continuity.

---

## 8. Dynamic Department & Subagent Hierarchy

1. **Elastic Scaling Bounds:**  
   Dynamic departments and subagents spawned under high workload are bounded by the safety limits defined in `config.py` (maximum 4 workers per department, maximum 12 concurrent subagents enterprise-wide).
2. **Ephemeral Lifecycle:**  
   Dynamic subagents possess no permanent independent state and are automatically retired when their assigned task queues are cleared.
